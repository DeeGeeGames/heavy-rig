# Square Slate Rook

The owner selected Square Slate as the Raider concept on November 22, 2026. The original raster and request remain the visual source. I retained the cream chest with its slate brow, small amber viewport, twin shoulder intakes, low arm trunnions, forward knees and paired ivory toe caps. Spare space under the dorsal rack exposes its two supports. The gun-side cheeks and the recessed slots in the autocannon cuff carry the original side profile. [Approved concept](../concept/a/image.png) · [Matched comparison](family-review.png)

The first neutral assembly brought the feet and gun housings too close together. I widened the stance, opened the arm spacing, lengthened the gun envelope and compared the revision at the same subject height. Its eight dorsal wells preserve the approved face. The two-round cycle uses the inner lower and opposite inner upper wells. The concealed rear radiator, sole construction and individual pivot positions are delegated design decisions for this model.
