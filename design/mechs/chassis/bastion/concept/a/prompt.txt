ARMOR AND COLOR AT GAMEPLAY SIZE

Build the silhouette from a few recognizable masses. Give the cockpit, weapon housings and legs distinct profiles when viewed from above and in three-quarter view. Shape each armor shell with broad planar faces and a few deliberate bevels. Leave room to see where a limb meets its pivot and where a weapon ends.

Choose a compact chassis palette. Carry the main paint color across large uninterrupted armor faces, group a second color on a few whole panels or housings, and recess the joints and weapon openings into a darker value. Use a small bright visor as a focal point. Let a change of plane catch one broad highlight. The face of a launcher can show its grouped openings while its sides stay quiet.

At the size of the mechs in the attached Practice range view, the cockpit, stance and fitted weapon families should still read.
