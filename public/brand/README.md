# Heavy Rig title artwork

## Field Standard logo

`heavy-rig.png` is the approved Field Standard revision with closer hip attachments,
without the Frontier Company subtitle. Generated with built-in imagegen and selected
by the owner on 2026-11-09. Original export: 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG.

The title screen scales this transparent source without altering its artwork.

## Meridian Dusk

`meridian-dusk.webp` is the pixel-art landscape approved on 2026-11-24.
The original Meridian relay-yard painting and its pixel-art treatment were created
with built-in ImageGen. The lossless WebP combines the 416 × 234 plate with the
prototype's hand-animated tracers, flashes, and smoke in a 16-second loop.
