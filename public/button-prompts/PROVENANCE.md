# Button glyph provenance

These SVGs are copied without modification from DeeGee Games' Math Marsh
repository (`math-game`, revision `8c8ab078b5e951cd93973c176488783ff6ce328f`):

- `src/assets/button-prompts/`: confirm, back, and menu/options glyphs.
- `art/reference/button-prompts/`: remaining face-button and keyboard glyphs.

The same glyph family is used by DeeGee Games' All or Nothing under
`src/components/button-prompts/assets/`. Reuse was explicitly requested by the
owner for Heavy Rig's controller menu redesign.

No upstream author or separate license declaration accompanies these SVG files
in either inspected source repository. This file records the source; it does
not assign an unverified third-party license or claim the assets are CC0.

Shoulder-button and other missing glyphs are rendered as platform-specific text
keycaps in `src/input-prompts.ts`; they do not use additional third-party art.
