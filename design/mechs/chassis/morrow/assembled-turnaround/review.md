# Bulwark Morrow

I selected the hock-corrected Bulwark from the earlier low carrier, upright Twin Tower and swept Manta explorations under the owner’s delegated creative authority. The low cockpit bridge makes the two olive receiver bodies the strongest shapes. The built-in ImageGen edit kept the buff collars and short forward feet and replaced the six holes on each exploratory nose with four larger guided wells. [Edit input](../concept/a/exploration.png) · [Selected concept](../concept/a/image.png) · [Family review](family-review.png)

Two short, double-supported trunnions connect the side receivers to the yawing bridge. The aft shells and blank feed doors stay on the shared body. Each removable firing cassette completes the forward half. The upper legs turn back toward high rear hocks and the shins return forward to broad feet. I brought the amber windscreen forward and inclined it below its buff brow after the first range-angle render obscured the pilot glass.

| Variant | Cassette on each side | Total active wells |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salvo | Broad Trident/4 face with two rows of two | 8 |
| Saturation | Wide, shortened Hailstorm/6 face with two rows of three | 12 |
| Pursuer | Long, narrow Needle/2 face with one vertical pair | 4 |

The front and elevated views show the changing cassette widths. The side views show the shared rear receivers and reverse-jointed load path. A far-side pod can be fully occluded in profile. The A concept depicts a legal part-load of 240 rounds per pod. The stock game package uses 360 per pod inside the same housing.
