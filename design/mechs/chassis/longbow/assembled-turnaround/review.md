# Spire Longbow

I selected Spire from the earlier silhouette exploration under the owner’s delegated creative authority. Its high narrow pilot cell, single vertical rear cooler and low own-left rifle distinguish it from the flatter Lance and broad-backed Bastion concepts. The built-in ImageGen edit preserved the plum and bone construction and added a recessed violet ceramic emitter visible at the end of the accelerator. The source image, ordered reference inputs and exact submitted prompt are retained together. [Edit input](../concept/a/exploration.png) · [Selected concept](../concept/a/image.png) · [Family review](family-review.png)

The game geometry uses a rigid cooler cabinet with rear and side louvers, a protected amber viewport, a linked waist and long forward-jointed legs. The first fitted model compressed the arm in the range view. I widened the separation at the elbows, broadened the particle window and extended the main weapon. The small opposite maintenance clamp remains part of the arm. Rifleman and Overwatch fit the same independent load-bearing brace around it.

| Variant | Own-left | Own-right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marksman | Tempest in its bone-tipped cradle | Closed clamp |
| Rifleman | AC/38 with side feed cover and long barrel | Fixed brace and compact Arc lens |
| Overwatch | Marksman Tempest | Rifleman brace and two guided Needle wells |

The own-left profile exposes the long accelerator and the taller cooler behind the cockpit. Across the front views, the right clamp, compact lens and twin wells distinguish the three fits. The opposite profile can hide the far-side weapon. The stock Overwatch request carries 240 rounds for the compact guided launcher.
